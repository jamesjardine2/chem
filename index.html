    Heat Lab
    Arrow keys move · Enter drop · Del remove

    How to use the lab

    • Drag anything from the shelf onto the bench, a heater, or into a pot.
    • Stack things on each other to move heat between them.
    • Slide a heater left for cold, right for hot.
    • Drag a thermometer onto anything to read its temperature and draw its graph line.
    • Pour one container onto another to mix liquids.
    • Liquids always need a container.

    The rules this lab follows

    • Heat energy always moves from hotter to colder. Cold never flows.
    • Energy is never used up. It only moves, and the totals always add up.
    • While something melts, freezes, boils or condenses, its temperature stays flat.
